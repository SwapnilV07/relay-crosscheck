How this was worked out

  • Error rate = failed or refunded ÷ (completed + failed + refunded). Requests still in progress are left out of the rate.
  • "Likely owner" maps each Relay fail reason to who can fix it, using Relay's Handling Execution Errors docs. It's a support heuristic, not an official classification.
  • A day stands out when its rate is at least 1.5× and 2 points above the same chain's other days. A chain is flagged when an owner's error rate is at least double, and 3 points above, the integrator's other chains.
  • Label other: Not classified by the source.
  • Label relay_failure: Labelled as a Relay failure by the integrator.
  • Label sponsorship: Assumed to mean the integrator's fee-sponsorship App Balance ran short (SPONSOR_BALANCE_TOO_LOW). The source does not define this label; confirm with the integrator.
